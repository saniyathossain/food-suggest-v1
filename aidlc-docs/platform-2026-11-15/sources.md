## Content corrected against these sources (migration 0007 + seed)
- Migraine: cheese, sausage, tasting salt, instant noodles, soft drinks moved from **avoid** to **go easy** — the American
  Migraine Foundation states there are no universal food triggers and that avoiding every possible trigger is unhealthy;
  reasons now say "keep a diary, avoid it if it triggers you".
- Diabetes: bitter gourd "may help a little; never replaces medicine"; "don't eat fruit with rice" became the tip
  "have fruit as a separate snack".
- Fatty liver: coffee only black, unsweetened, 2–3 cups, if the doctor agrees.
- Thyroid: tablet 30–60 min before breakfast (NHS, unchanged); calcium / iron / soy at least 2 hours apart, 4 if you can
  (NHS; was a flat "4 hours").
