Scope: whole Pathya web app (home, condition, plan, foods, admin). Mode: Operate (home leans Persuade). Code-led.
Audience: adults in Bangladesh managing diabetes, fatty liver, asthma, migraine, thyroid, insomnia; often on mid-range Android, daylight, bilingual EN/বাংলা.
Job: pick profile, read what to eat / limit / avoid and when; admin curates content.
Constraints: fast, light-first (dark optional), real food photography from media library, macOS-style liquid-glass dock + top bar kept.
User rejected: dark Golden Gate wallpaper, glass on every card, purple haze.

## Direction contract
THESIS: The category standard played straight at Lifesum's craft level — a bright, food-first nutrition app — refusing the moody wallpaper-and-glass treatment; glass lives only in the dock and top bar, as on macOS.
OWN-WORLD: White ground with a faint green-gray page tint (#f6f8f5), crisp white cards with soft 1px borders and low shadows, Lifesum-fresh green brand (#1fae5b) as the one accent; verdict colours red/amber/blue/green only on chips. Figtree for UI and headings, Hind Siliguri for Bangla. 20–24px radii, full-bleed food photos on cards.
STORY: Visitor sees appetising Bangladeshi food, understands "tell us your conditions, get one plan", builds it in one card, then browses conditions by photo.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Left: large heading + short lead + profile builder card with green primary button. Right: tall photo of the next meal with a floating "next meal" pill. Below fold: condition photo cards in a 3-column grid. Liquid-glass dock floating at bottom centre.
FORM: canon (user-chosen standard, reference Lifesum). seed: none (pinned) — user: "revamp entire looks with industry standard food nutrition like sites", "mac doc like blur effect", chose Lifesum, light first.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
