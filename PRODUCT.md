# Pathya (পথ্য)

*Pathya* — Bangla for "diet suited to a patient". Food that fits your body, the Bangladeshi way.

People in Bangladesh living with diabetes, fatty liver, asthma, migraine, thyroid problems or insomnia get
conflicting advice from family, pharmacies and social media. Pathya gives one calm, bilingual answer: which everyday
Bangladeshi foods to eat more of, go easy on or avoid, when to eat them, and which habits help — combined across
all of a person's conditions, with the safer rule winning when they disagree.

Tone: a kind nutritionist. Never diagnoses, always defers to the person's doctor.
Feel: macOS calm — frosted glass, a dock, a Golden Gate sunset; fast on a mid-range Android phone.
